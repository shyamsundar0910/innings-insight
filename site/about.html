About the analyst

Shyamsundar Nandakumar

I’m a Business Intelligence & Data Analyst with a background in computer science and analytics. I earned my Master of Science in Analytics from Northeastern University and my Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering from SRM Institute of Science and Technology. Today, I work as an analyst, using data, visualization, automation and statistical thinking to turn complex information into clearer decisions.

Cricket is where that analytical instinct becomes personal. I have always loved watching the game, but over time I started looking beyond the scorecard — at phases, matchups, role changes, bowling combinations, venue behaviour, pressure points and tactical decisions.

I still watch cricket as a fan. I just happen to watch it with an analytical lens.

Innings Insight is the space where those two interests meet. I use cricket data to test the questions I naturally ask while watching a match, build analytical projects around them, and turn the findings into stories that are useful and easy to discuss. The goal is not to make cricket feel mechanical; it is to understand the game more deeply through evidence.

Whether I am studying a player, a team transition, a powerplay pairing or a venue, I start with a cricket question and let the numbers challenge the assumption.
