About the analyst

Shyamsundar Nandakumar

I’m an analyst who enjoys turning complex data into clear, useful insights. I earned a Master of Science in Analytics from Northeastern University and a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering from SRM Institute of Science and Technology. I currently work as an analyst, applying data, reporting and business intelligence to real-world problems.

Cricket has always been more than a game I watch. Over time, my background in analytics changed the way I see it — I began looking beyond the scorecard to patterns in phases, matchups, player roles, venue behaviour and tactical decisions. That curiosity became Innings Insight, where I use data to explore cricket questions and turn the numbers behind the game into stories that are easier to understand.

I still watch cricket as a fan first, but now I also watch it through an analytical lens.

That means asking why a phase changed, which matchup created an edge, what the data says about a decision, and whether the story we see on the field is supported by the numbers.
