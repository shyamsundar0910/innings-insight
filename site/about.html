About the analyst

Shyamsundar Nandakumar

Innings Insight is where I combine cricket curiosity with data analysis. I build projects that look past the final score and focus on the patterns underneath — phase performance, player matchups, tactical control, venue behaviour and long-term team trends.

My work uses Python, SQL, visualization and statistical thinking, but the goal is always simple: turn numbers into a cricket story that is useful, understandable and worth discussing.

The question comes first. The data decides whether the story survives.

Alongside interactive projects, I publish long-form analysis on Substack and visual breakdowns on Instagram.
