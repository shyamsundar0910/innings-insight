Hardik Pandya: captaincy beyond win percentage.

A comparison of how his teams controlled different phases of an IPL innings, and what changed when the environment around the captain changed.

Explore projectsRead more on Substack
The question

What does captaincy control look like in the numbers?

Win percentage is useful, but it can hide where a team is actually gaining or losing control. This project breaks matches into phases and compares the underlying scoring and bowling patterns around Hardik Pandya’s captaincy spells.

Analytical lens

  • Powerplay scoring and wicket control.
  • Middle-over run suppression and wicket-taking.
  • Death-over execution under pressure.
  • Differences between team environments rather than treating every season as directly comparable.

What the analysis suggested

The strongest contrast was not simply wins versus losses. The more interesting story appeared in how consistently the bowling unit controlled the middle overs and whether that control survived into the death. The Gujarat environment showed a clearer phase structure, while the Mumbai period exposed more volatility when middle-over control weakened and death-over costs rose.

That does not make every outcome a captaincy outcome. Squad construction, bowling resources, venue, opposition and player form all matter. The project therefore treats captaincy as one layer of the explanation rather than the only one.

Why this format works

Breaking the game into phases makes tactical differences easier to interpret. Instead of saying a side “bowled badly,” the analysis can identify whether the damage came from an ineffective powerplay, a lack of middle-over pressure, or expensive death execution.
