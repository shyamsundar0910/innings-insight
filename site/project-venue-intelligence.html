Venue intelligence

Build a venue profile, not just an average score.

Venue Intelligence is being designed to describe how a ground actually plays by combining phase scoring, bowling style, toss effects and winning-score context.

In development

Why venue averages are not enough.

An average first-innings score gives only one layer of information. Two venues can have the same average and still reward completely different strategies. One may favour pace in the powerplay and spin in the middle; another may become dramatically easier to chase at night.

The intended output

The end product will be an interactive venue card that answers practical questions: what bowling style has worked here, where teams score fastest, how much chasing changes the result, and what range of totals has historically been competitive.

Current status

The analysis framework is defined and the data-cleaning stage is being built, including venue-name standardisation and more reliable classification of bowling types. The page will be expanded as the project moves from prototype to interactive tool.
